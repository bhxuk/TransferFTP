What it does

Everything a file mover should do.

Built for moving websites, backups, media and configs between the places you keep them.

SFTPSCPFTPFTPS explicitFTPS implicitNetwork shares

Drag between anything

PC to server, server to PC, or server straight to server. Drop onto a folder to put files inside it. Hold Shift to move.

A queue that copes

Several files at once with speed and time left. Pause and resume, and dropped connections reconnect and carry on from the same byte.

Edit, save, done

Open a file on a server in your own editor. Every save uploads automatically, so quick fixes take seconds.

Safe by default

Passwords are encrypted for your Windows account. Server keys are checked and remembered, and certificate problems are shown before you trust them.

Keys, not just passwords

OpenSSH and PuTTY keys with passphrases, plus keyboard-interactive sign-in for servers that need it.

Tabs and windows

Keep several servers and folders open on each side, or open a second window. Your tabs come back next time.

Quick links

Save the folders you use most, on your PC or any server, and jump back in one click. Recent folders are listed too.

Space at a glance

Free space on the drive, share or server under each side, and folder sizes with one click, subfolders included.

One-click updates

When a new version is out, an Update button appears. One click installs it and reopens with your tabs as they were.

Help on hover

Every button says what it does and its shortcut when you hover over it.

Bring your sites

Import saved sites from FileZilla in one click. Test a connection before you save it.

Compare and sync

See every change before it happens.

Point the two panes at two folders and press Sync. TransferFTP lists every file it would copy, create or delete, and nothing moves until you say so.

  • Left to right, right to left, or both ways with the newer file winning
  • Compare by size and date, or size only
  • Skip lists like .git;node_modules;*.tmp
  • Deletes are off unless you turn them on, and always confirmed

SSH terminal

A shell, right where you are.

Press Terminal, pick a server, and you're in. It opens in the folder you're looking at, so restarting a service after an upload is one step.

  • Full colour, with nano, vim, htop and less working inside
  • Select to copy, right-click to paste, Ctrl+C to stop a command
  • Scroll back through earlier output, and resize the text

Tabs

Every new tab asks where to go.

Press + or Ctrl+T and pick This PC, the place you're already in, any saved site, one of your quick links, or a brand new connection. Type to find a site in a long list.

  • Sites already open are marked Connected
  • Add a site and open it in the same step
  • Tabs come back next time you start TransferFTP

Network shares

Every share on a machine, in one place.

Add a Windows PC or NAS by name, even over Tailscale, and leave the share blank. TransferFTP lists every shared drive with its free space, just like Explorer.

  • Double-click a share to open it, Up to go back to the list
  • Free space shows for each share and for the folder you're in
  • Clear messages when a sign-in or firewall gets in the way

Updates

New versions in one click.

When there's an update, a button appears at the top of the window. Click it and TransferFTP downloads the new version, installs it and opens again where you left off. Or let it update itself while nothing is transferring.

Get started

Connected in a minute.

Install

Run TransferFTP-Setup.exe. It installs just for you, no admin rights needed.

Add a site

Press Sites, choose the type, add the host and sign-in, and test it.

Drag files

Open it on one side, your PC on the other, and drag. The queue does the rest.
